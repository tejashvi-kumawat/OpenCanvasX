# Personas and use cases

> **Source:** Derived from [Open_Design_Studio_Complete_Specification.md](../Open_Design_Studio_Complete_Specification.md).
> **Status:** Normative product/architecture documentation for Open Design Studio.

## Primary audiences

| Persona | Goals | Key features |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| Student / creator | Social graphics, posters, offline school work | Templates, PNG/PDF, free assets |
| Freelancer | Client deliverables, brand consistency | Brand kit, SVG/PDF, local files |
| Small team | Shared templates, optional live edit | P2P collab, comments, self-hosted relay |
| Organization | On-prem control | Optional relay, private asset/template repos |

## Use case categories (from product scope)

## 1. PRODUCT POSITIONING

Primary positioning:

"Professional design software that is free, local-first, private, open, and extensible."

The application should cover:

- Graphic design
- Social media design
- Presentations
- Documents
- Posters
- Flyers
- Resumes
- Certificates
- Invitations
- Business cards
- Infographics
- Whiteboards
- Diagrams
- Mind maps
- Charts
- Simple video compositions
- Simple motion graphics
- Brand kits
- Publishing
- PDF export
- SVG export
- PNG/JPEG/WebP export
- PPTX-compatible workflows where technically feasible
- Template-based design
- Community templates
- Asset management
- Local AI
- BYOK AI
- Agentic design workflows
- Real-time P2P collaboration
